About Ayush Menat Foundation

Inform • Educate • Empower • Build a Better Society

Who We Are

A foundation for information, education and social welfare

Ayush Menat Foundation works with a single belief — an informed society is a strong society. From our base in Kunol, Meghraj, Aravalli (Gujarat), we reach out to families who lack access to authentic information, quality learning and basic welfare support.

Our logo — an open book, a globe, a pen, a camera and a rising family — reflects our work in education, news and media, community and nature.

Mission & Vision

Our guiding light

Our Mission

To create an informed, educated and empowered society through awareness, information dissemination and social welfare activities for the benefit of all, especially the poor, economically weaker, marginalized and underprivileged communities.

Our Vision

To be a leading foundation in India that inspires healthier, more aware and empowered communities through education, information, awareness and social development.
