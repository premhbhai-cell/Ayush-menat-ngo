About Us

Building an informed, educated and empowered society

Ayush Menat Foundation is a social organisation based in Kunol, Meghraj (Aravalli, Gujarat). We bring authentic information, education support and awareness to people who need it the most.

  • Authentic information for public benefit
  • Seminars, workshops & learning support
  • Health, hygiene and environment awareness
Know More →
Mission & Vision

What drives us every day

Our Mission

To create an informed, educated and empowered society through awareness, information dissemination and social welfare activities for the benefit of all, especially the poor, economically weaker, marginalized and underprivileged communities.

Our Vision

To be a leading foundation in India that inspires healthier, more aware and empowered communities through education, information, awareness and social development.

Our Focus Areas

Empowering People, Strengthening Communities

Six pillars through which we serve society.

News & Information

Authentic, verified information shared for public benefit — government schemes, rights, and local updates.

Education

Seminars, workshops and learning support that open doors to a brighter future.

Social Welfare

Support for poor, disadvantaged and underprivileged communities.

Health Awareness

Promoting health, hygiene and well-being for all through camps and awareness drives.

Environment

Awareness and initiatives for a cleaner and greener tomorrow — tree plantation, cleanliness drives.

Community Development

Building stronger, happier and more inclusive communities together.
